Zikr Counter - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Zikr Counter ("the app", by Ghufran Apps) is a tasbih / dhikr counter. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no login, no advertising and no analytics in this version.

Data stored on your phone

Your counts, dhikr list, settings and daily totals are saved only on your own phone. They never leave your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed with it.

Volume-button counting (optional)

This feature is off by default. It starts only after you choose "Turn on" in the app. When it is on, the volume buttons count your dhikr instead of changing the volume, and this also works while the screen is off or locked. The app only registers that a volume button was pressed. It does not read, record or send anything else.

  • An ongoing notification is shown while the feature is on.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Settings or on the Counter screen.
  • During phone calls, or while another app plays audio or video, counting pauses automatically and the volume buttons control the normal volume again.
  • With the screen off and no use for about one minute, the app stops listening to save battery.
  • To receive volume-button presses while the screen is off, Android requires an active audio session. The app therefore plays a silent (zero-volume) audio stream, only while the screen is off and only when you have turned this feature on. No sound is audible and no audio or microphone input is recorded.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show a small ongoing notification while volume-button counting is active.
  • Foreground service (special use): to keep optional volume-button counting working while the screen is off, only after you turn it on. It is used only for this counting.
  • Vibration: to give a short vibration at the end of a round or when you reach a target.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
